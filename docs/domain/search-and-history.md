# Search and history

## Searching for anime

Type a title to search your list service. Results show the same information as your list, and you can add any of them to your list directly.

Everything you find through search is remembered by Shiori. That matters for recognition: Shiori can only recognize files of anime it has seen. See [Recognizing the anime](recognition.md).

### Filters

Results can be narrowed by year, season, type (TV, film, OVA and so on) and airing status (airing, finished, not yet aired), and sorted by title, episode length, score, type or start date.

### Browsing a season

Choosing both a season and a year, without typing anything, lists everything that started airing in that season. Shiori loads the whole season, not just the first page.

Seasons follow the usual anime calendar: winter is December to February, spring March to May, summer June to August, fall September to November. December counts toward the following year's winter.

## Viewing your list and search results

Both can be shown as a table or as cards with cover images. In the table you can sort by any column; ties are broken by a second column you choose and then by title. A text box filters by title and matches any of an anime's titles.

Shiori remembers the view, the sorting and the filters you last used.

## Anime details

Opening an anime shows its cover, all its titles, type, episode count and length, airing status, season, average score, genres, tags, studios, producers and synopsis. From the same window you edit your list entry and the anime's own settings: your alternative titles, its folder, and your display title.

## History

The history lists every episode marked as watched, newest first: the anime, the episode, and when. Both automatic updates and progress you set by hand appear in it.

You can remove single lines or clear the whole history. The history is a log only: removing a line does not change your list.

The history is kept on your computer and is not sent to the list service.

## Waiting changes

Next to the history you see the changes that have not reached the list service yet. See [Synchronizing with your list service](sync.md).
